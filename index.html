PIROBA

Put money on your promises.

Friends stake USDC on their goals. Keep your promise and you get your stake back plus a share of what the others forfeited. პირობა means "promise" in Georgian.

View on GitHub

How it works

01

Create

Pick a goal, a duration, a stake (e.g. 20 USDC) and a proof method. The rules lock when the first person joins.

02

Join

Friends open a link and stake with a wallet or just an email. Fees are sponsored, so nobody needs SOL.

03

Prove

Check in daily or weekly with a private zkTLS proof from apps like Strava, or with a photo the group votes on.

04

Settle

After the deadline, finishers get their stake back plus a share of the forfeits, and a badge that can't be sold or transferred.

Why Solana

THE BANK

Nobody holds the money

Stakes sit in a program-owned USDC escrow that no person, including us, can move.

THE REFEREE

Fair, visible rules

The program verifies proofs and records votes and disputes on-chain, so everyone sees the same result.

THE SCOREBOARD

Proof you did it

Every finisher gets a non-transferable Token-2022 badge, a permanent record that can't be bought.
